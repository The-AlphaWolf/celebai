## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `polished` with `app-store` energy
- Creative direction: High-end tech product launch meets celebration festivity
- Interpretation: Precise typography, clean glassmorphic surfaces, dynamic color palette shifts, and rhythmic cuts.
- Angle: Replacing fragmented spreadsheets and wedding/party planning stress with instant, structured AI architecture.
- Hook: High-contrast problem statement slam into the radiant CelebAI brand reveal.
- Outro / punchline: "Plan less. Celebrate more."
