# Brag Plan: CelebAI — GenAI Celebration & Event Planner

## What is this app?
CelebAI is a GenAI-powered event planning studio that instantly turns chaotic celebration ideas into production-grade master plans for weddings, milestone birthdays, and community festivals with dynamic budget charts, phased timelines, vendor checklists, and bespoke design themes.

## The angle
Event planning is notoriously stressful, fragmented across spreadsheets, notes, and panic. CelebAI replaces that chaos with instant, structured intelligence powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash: visual budgets that calculate allocations in real time, interactive phased timelines, categorized vendor trackers, and 5 bespoke celebration aesthetics.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Event planning is pure chaos. Until now."  
Fast typography slam into the glowing brand reveal: **CelebAI — Built with Gemini 2.5 Flash**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Event Spectrum:** 1-click instant switching between Wedding, Birthday, and Community Gatherings.
- **The Visual Budget Donut:** Dynamic conic-gradient financial breakdown allocating venue, catering, photo, and music in real time.
- **The Phased Execution Timeline:** From 6 months out to day-of hourly execution with interactive checkpoint toggles.
- **5 Bespoke Design Themes:** Seamlessly transforming the entire UI on the fly (Champagne Royal, Emerald Sanctuary, Cyberpunk Synthwave, Pearl Aurora).
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash Copilot:** Instant AI refinement and one-click PDF Dossier export.

## Outro / punchline
"Plan less. Celebrate more."  
Closing brand card with PromptWars Hackathon badge, Gemini 2.5 Flash seal, and clear call-to-action.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Select celebration archetype (Wedding / Birthday / Community) or launch custom intake.
2. **Key Action:** Live financial budget reallocation & interactive milestone checkoff.
3. **Refinement & Delivery:** 1-click theme switch and one-click PDF Dossier export.
